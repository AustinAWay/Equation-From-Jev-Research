## Exact-input counterexample, during reference collection

A read-only audit of old repeat-consistent data found 5 conflicting groups of exactly identical selected-ledger measurement vectors among 389 steps. No rounding or approximate matching was used. For example, “Draw a perpendicular line at the marked point” had stable Jev-based target3, while “Examine two handwritten copies of a short passage” had stable target1, yet all30 local ledger measurements matched. A deterministic function of those30 values cannot output both recorded numbers. This is a precise representation limitation, independent of equation length. It is not proof that a95% target is impossible, nor that an equation using richer inputs cannot work. No frozen rule or prediction was changed. Full evidence is retained in diagnostics/exact_collisions.json.
