# What the reference flags mean

Suggested report wording (three sentences):

> A provisional score still counts the app's current groups and any extra connections that pass its checks, but some relationship decisions remain unresolved, so the number may change and is not a proven lower bound. A coverage warning applies to the whole passage—for example, only 12 earlier candidate ideas may be included in explicit checks—so copying that warning onto every reading step does not mean every step failed. We retain these available tentative scores; a missing primary grouping stays missing rather than being replaced with zero.

## Exact meanings in the frozen implementation

**Per-step `provisional` has a specific, narrower definition than general uncertainty.** A count must be available, and either the scenario's `relationalAssessable` or the segment's `relational_memory.assessable` must be false. Uncertain primary choices, a low-confidence label, an unreviewed grouping, or a passage coverage warning alone do not set this particular Boolean. See `web/src/lib/scenarios.js:61` (availability) and `:71` (provisional definition), then `web/src/lib/load-estimates.js:132` (per-step count/flag).

**Unresolved relational accounting is not just candidate truncation.** Each proposed connection starts as unknown. It remains unknown if its answer is absent, unavailable or explicitly unknown; if the separate confirmation is contradicted, uncertain or unavailable; or if the claimed existing representation cannot be resolved. Any such unknown makes relational accounting unassessable. Bounded alias lists can make the accounting uncertain, but do not by themselves force `assessable=false`. See `backend/relational_memory.py:220`, especially `:239`, `:245`, `:248`, and `:287`; the frontend independently reconstructs these rules in `web/src/lib/relational-memory.js:98` and `:118`. The final backend assembly requires confirmation (`backend/analysis.py:443`). There is no separate passage-level `unresolved` Boolean in the exported metadata; unresolved decisions contribute to per-step relational flags and passage-wide confidence explanations.

**The available count is primary groups plus accepted extra connection units.** Each existing group is counted once. A connection already represented by a group or another connection does not add a duplicate. An extra unit must originate from a supported separate-connection decision and survive the confirmation/schema validation; the support gate requires confidence at least 0.55, selected probability at least 0.60, and a leading margin at least 0.15. Weak proposed extras remain reviewable but are excluded from the number. Unknown connections do not receive an invented default relation or an automatic added unit: the current count remains available but incomplete/provisional. This must not be described as establishing zero cost for the unknown relation. See `web/src/lib/scenarios.js:31`, `web/src/lib/relational-memory.js:5`, `:33`, `:40`, and `backend/relational_memory.py:248`. The app explicitly says this provisional estimate is **not a lower bound** (`web/src/lib/load-estimates.js:20`).

**There is a separate primary-group fallback worth disclosing.** A valid primary choice of unknown, or an unusable grouping anchor, can retain the source occurrence as a tentative separate representation; tentative coherent joins can also remain grouped. Thus “no fallback whatsoever” would be inaccurate, although there is no default relational replacement. In contrast, an active-role service decision marked unavailable makes the primary grouping unassessable and its automatic count null. See `backend/memory.py:22`, `:52`, `:79`, and `:142`; `web/src/lib/scenarios.js:64` enforces the null count. The research scorer uses newly created, unconfirmed scenarios, not educator overrides.

**Passage-level `coverage_limited` means at least one coverage note exists.** Triggers include parser/language/notation limitations, a candidate crossing a reading-step boundary, no reliable candidates, missing learner context, spoken presentation, bounded prior-candidate or grouping-anchor checks, whole-clause rather than localized evidence, or unavailable service decisions after retry. See `backend/extraction.py:151`, `:250`, and `:268`; `backend/analysis.py:238`, `:244`, `:259`, `:463`, and `:475`; `backend/prompts.py:166`, `:293`, and `:371`. The 12-candidate rule narrows explicit earlier-occurrence checks while retaining the full available prefix in the request (`backend/prompts.py:382`). Relational uncertainty alone does not automatically add a passage coverage note. A general “English prose coverage is provisional” warning also does not itself set this flag: it is outside `coverage_notes` (`backend/extraction.py:152`).

**The research export propagates flags conservatively.** `score_analysis.mjs:29` saves each isolated step's available frontend count and provisional flag; `:34` copies the same passage-level coverage flag into every step row. `collect.py:231` and `:232` mark an aggregate step if any of its three repetitions was provisional or coverage-limited. Therefore 2,100 flagged development rows means those rows belonged to at least one repeated analysis with a passage coverage warning, not that 2,100 individual steps independently hit a cap or failed. Whole-passage low-confidence reasons are also separate from count availability (`web/src/lib/load-estimates.js:52` and `:143`).

The frontend contains a legacy scenario-construction fallback (`web/src/lib/load-estimates.js:96`), but the research scorer supplies explicit scenarios or explicit nulls for every step (`score_analysis.mjs:17`), so that fallback does not silently replace a failed score.
