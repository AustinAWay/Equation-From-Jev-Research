# Fresh 100-passage validation set

This set contains exactly 100 AI-authored educational passages in 50 paired scenario families. It is intended for a fresh check of an already frozen equation, with three independent Jev runs per passage. No new predictions or reference answers were viewed during authoring, and no service calls were made by the authoring worker.

The sample gives ten passages to each of ten areas: physical science, earth and geography, living systems, mathematics and data, digital technology, history and archives, community systems, language and literature, music and visual arts, and everyday design. Each area has one family in each of five writing styles: explanation, observational narrative, comparison/interpretation, worked reasoning, and procedure. Styles rotate across lengths. Each area has two short, two medium, and one longer family. Actual lengths are 53–62, 84–101, and 131–143 words respectively.

Each family is an explicitly written situation. Its second version changes a relation, reference, instruction, definition location, intervening information, sentence structure, or grouping description. The paired variants share context intentionally; they are not separate independent scenarios. Some changes preserve meaning, while others change the information required by a task. No change has an assumed numerical score or guaranteed direction. There are ten passages for each of the ten challenge labels. Domain and challenge labels are sampling metadata, not instructions to route the equation differently.

All needed facts and arbitrary rules are introduced in the text, using accessible classroom language. The cases were written as full situations; no common passage template was expanded across families by replacing nouns. The small literal replacements in author.py create only the two intended variants within a family.

The overlap check compares every new passage with the 788 unique historical texts in the requested sources, and compares all 4,950 pairs within this new set. Intentional within-family matches are included and marked. It checks normalized exact matches and phrase/character similarity. The closest historical matches were also read: they share generic wording or broad subject matter, but use different situations and tasks. The report records the thresholds, source hashes, and matches.

This is a balanced challenge sample, not a random sample of real textbooks or independently human-authored material. The author has worked on earlier corpora. Low wording overlap cannot prove complete conceptual independence, and 100 passages represent 50 paired scenario groups. A result measures agreement with the specified Jev app procedure; it does not itself validate a measurement of human memory. No item was chosen, edited, or removed because of a new model or reference score.
